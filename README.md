# Kyamwiru Lineage

> "The Kyamwiru family has lasted for over decades and still going on."

A professional family legacy website celebrating the Kyamwiru Lineage — a family rooted in Sembabule District, Kankalangye, Uganda. Built with vanilla HTML, CSS, and JavaScript. No frameworks, no build step, just clean handcrafted code.

---



## About

The Kyamwiru Lineage website is a digital home for our extended family — grandparents, uncles, aunties, brothers, sisters, grandsons, and granddaughters. It preserves our story, our faith, and our heritage for future generations.

- Location: Sembabule District, Kankalangye, Uganda
- Founded: Over six decades ago
- Generations: Four and counting

---

## Features

- Elegant, professional design inspired by African heritage
- Fully responsive layout for phones, tablets, and desktops
- Fast loading with lazy-loaded images and throttled scroll effects
- Family galleries for elders, aunties, and grandchildren
- Family Moments section with embedded videos
- Family article telling the story of the lineage
- Smooth animations including hero zoom, scroll reveals, and animated counters
- Single-page application routing for instant navigation
- Accessible and semantic HTML
